# 🛡️ WebSafe Research AI

### AI-Powered Website Safety Research & Risk Analysis

WebSafe Research AI is an AI-powered research tool designed to analyze publicly observable website signals and provide a clear, structured safety assessment.

It helps users understand potential security and trust-related indicators before browsing, creating an account, sharing personal information, or making a payment.

## 🔎 What It Analyzes

- 🔒 HTTPS & SSL status
- 📡 HTTP response status
- 🔁 Redirect behavior
- 🛡️ Security-related signals
- 🌐 Domain-related signals
- ⚠️ Suspicious indicators and red flags
- 🎯 Risk assessment
- 📊 Confidence level
- 🧠 Trust Reasoning
- 👤 Practical user guidance

## 🧠 Trust Reasoning

WebSafe Research AI does more than provide a risk label.

It follows a simple:

**Evidence → Reasoning → User Guidance**

approach to explain why certain website signals may indicate lower or higher risk.
